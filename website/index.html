Questions

What do I need to run it?

An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later. You grant Screen Recording once (everything is processed on your Mac and never leaves it), then turn effects on.

Is my screen sent anywhere?

No. Frames are processed on the GPU and discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded. No accounts, no analytics.

What's the free tier?

The cinematic presets, applied as-is, with the intensity and quality controls. One $9.99 purchase unlocks the full library, editing, the composer, and Glass.

What is "Glass" and does it need anything special?

Glass adds live window transparency and a desktop tint. It's optional, and it's the only feature that needs part of System Integrity Protection disabled (a reversible, one-time Recovery step). Everything else works without it.
