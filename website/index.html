01

Capture

Questions

What do I need to run it?

An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later. You grant Screen Recording once (everything is processed on your Mac and never leaves it), then turn effects on.

Is my screen sent anywhere?

No. Frames are processed on the GPU and discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded. No accounts, no analytics.

What’s free, and what’s the $9.99?

Free gives you four full cinematic worlds — Golden Hour, Fuji-Film, Noir, and Cyberpunk — with the intensity and quality controls, with no time limit. One $9.99 purchase unlocks the other 12 worlds, all 169 effects, editing and building your own, and Glass.

What is “Glass” and does it need anything special?

Glass adds live window transparency and a desktop tint. It’s optional, and it’s the only feature that needs part of System Integrity Protection disabled (a reversible, one-time Recovery step). Everything else works without it.

Are these the real effects?

The worlds you see shading this page are WebGL ports of the actual presets. The Mac app runs the full Metal pipeline at native resolution over your whole desktop — sharper, faster, and with the other 165 effects behind them.
